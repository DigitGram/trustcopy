# Code of Conduct

This is a small project that wants to stay friendly. Everyone who takes part (issues, discussions, pull requests, translations) is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, also with newcomers and with people whose first language is not English,
- criticise code and ideas, never people,
- accept that the author decides what goes into TrustCopy, and that "no" or "not now" is a normal answer.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing other people's private information, and spam.

The author may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing it.

To report a problem, write to digitgram@proton.me (or use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment).

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
